## Per-Paper Annotations

### Gepner et al. (2025)

**Research question**: How can soft robotics research overcome challenges in prototyping, manufacturing, and standardization to accelerate translation from laboratory advancements to scalable applications?

**Methodology**: The authors present and demonstrate the Flex Printer, an open-source low-cost FDM platform that enables reliable printing of ultra-flexible soft robots with embedded fluidic logic. They employ an innovative upside-down print orientation to expand printable geometries and validate the approach by demonstrating a robot that autonomously walks off the print bed immediately after fabrication.

**Key findings**:
- The Flex Printer enables reliable printing of ultra-flexible soft robots with embedded fluidic logic using an open-source, low-cost FDM platform.
- An innovative upside-down print orientation significantly expands the range of printable geometries.
- The approach allows robots to autonomously walk off the print bed immediately after fabrication, representing a milestone achievement in soft robotics.
- This platform provides a foundation for standardization and scalable manufacturing in soft robotics.
- The technology has potential to democratize soft robotics research and lower barriers to entry for new applications.

**Limitations**: Not stated in abstract.
