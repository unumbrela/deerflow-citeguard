## Executive Summary

This systematic literature review examines recent advances in 3D and 4D world modeling, focusing on generative models, scene reconstruction, and world simulators for autonomous driving and embodied AI. The reviewed papers demonstrate a clear progression from static 3D scene generation to dynamic 4D representations that incorporate temporal evolution, physical constraints, and multimodal integration. Key themes include Gaussian splatting extensions to 4D, occupancy-based world simulators, compositional generative models for urban environments, and unified frameworks bridging appearance, geometry, and dynamics.

## Annotated Bibliography (APA Format)

### 1. 4D Language Gaussian Splatting

Li, W., Zhou, R., Zhou, J., Song, Y., Herter, J., Qin, M., Huang, G., & Pfister, H. (2025). *4D LangSplat: 4D Language Gaussian Splatting via Multimodal Large Language Models*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2503.10437. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10437v2

**Summary:** This paper extends LangSplat from 3D static scenes to dynamic 4D environments by learning 4D language fields that enable time-sensitive, open-ended language queries. The key innovation is bypassing vision features and instead learning directly from text generated by Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) through object-wise video captions. A multimodal object-wise video prompting method guides MLLMs to generate temporally consistent captions, which are encoded into sentence embeddings for open-vocabulary text queries. A status deformable network models smooth transitions across states in 4D scenes. Results demonstrate precise and efficient performance for both time-sensitive and time-agnostic queries in dynamic scenes.

**Key Contributions:**
- First work to extend language-grounded Gaussian splatting to 4D dynamic scenes
- Novel MLLM-based captioning approach for temporal consistency
- Status deformable network for continuous state transitions

### 2. 4D Spatial Intelligence Survey

Cao, Y., Lu, J., Huang, Z., Shen, Z., Zhao, C., Hong, F., Chen, Z., Li, X., Wang, W., Liu, Y., & Liu, Z. (2025). *Reconstructing 4D Spatial Intelligence: A Survey*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2507.21045. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21045v2

**Summary:** This comprehensive survey organizes 4D scene reconstruction methods into five progressive levels of spatial intelligence: (1) low-level 3D attributes (depth, pose, point maps), (2) 3D scene components (objects, humans, structures), (3) 4D dynamic scenes, (4) interaction modeling among components, and (5) physical laws and constraints. The authors address a gap in existing surveys by providing hierarchical analysis of 4D reconstruction, covering applications from entertainment (movies) to embodied AI (interaction modeling). The survey discusses key challenges at each level and highlights promising directions for advancing toward richer 4D spatial intelligence.

**Key Contributions:**
- First hierarchical framework organizing 4D reconstruction into five levels
- Comprehensive analysis spanning entertainment to embodied AI applications
- Identification of challenges and future directions at each intelligence level

### 3. Compositional 4D City Generation

Xie, H., Chen, Z., Hong, F., & Liu, Z. (2025). *Compositional Generative Model of Unbounded 4D Cities*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2501.08983. https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08983v4

**Summary:** CityDreamer4D addresses the challenge of generating realistic 4D cities by separating dynamic objects (vehicles) from static scenes (buildings, roads). The compositional model uses Traffic Scenario Generator and Unbounded Layout Generator to produce dynamic traffic scenarios and static city layouts in compact BEV representation. Objects are generated by combining stuff-oriented and instance-oriented neural fields with customized generative hash grids and periodic positional embeddings. The approach supports downstream applications including instance editing, city stylization, and urban simulation, achieving state-of-the-art performance in 4D city generation.

**Key Contributions:**
- Compositional architecture separating static and dynamic city components
- Customized neural fields for different object types (background, buildings, vehicles)
- Comprehensive dataset suite (OSM, GoogleEarth, CityTopia) for city generation

### 4. Occupancy-Based World Simulator for Autonomous Driving

Wang, L., Zheng, W., Ren, Y., Jiang, H., Cui, Z., Yu, H., & Lu, J. (2024). *OccSora: 4D Occupancy Generation Models as World Simulators for Autonomous Driving*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2405.20337. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20337v1

**Summary:** OccSora proposes a diffusion-based 4D occupancy generation model as a world simulator for autonomous driving, addressing inefficiency in autoregressive methods for long-term temporal modeling. The approach employs a 4D scene tokenizer for compact discrete spatial-temporal representations and learns a diffusion transformer to generate 4D occupancy conditioned on trajectory prompts. Experiments on nuScenes with Occ3D annotations demonstrate generation of 16-second videos with authentic 3D layout and temporal consistency. The model shows potential as a world simulator for autonomous driving decision-making through trajectory-aware 4D generation.

**Key Contributions:**
- Diffusion-based alternative to autoregressive 4D modeling
- 4D scene tokenizer for compact spatial-temporal representations
- Trajectory-conditioned generation for autonomous driving applications

### 5. 4D Embodied World Models

Zhen, H., Sun, Q., Zhang, H., Li, J., Zhou, S., Du, Y., & Gan, C. (2025). *TesserAct: Learning 4D Embodied World Models*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2504.20995. https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20995v1

**Summary:** TesserAct presents an approach for learning 4D embodied world models that predict dynamic evolution of 3D scenes in response to agent actions, ensuring spatial and temporal consistency. The method trains on RGB-DN (RGB, Depth, Normal) videos, surpassing 2D models by incorporating shape, configuration, and temporal changes. The approach extends robotic manipulation datasets with depth and normal information using off-the-shelf models, fine-tunes video generation to jointly predict RGB-DN frames, and converts generated videos into high-quality 4D scenes. The method enables novel view synthesis and facilitates policy learning outperforming prior video-based world models.

**Key Contributions:**
- RGB-DN video training for embodied 4D world prediction
- Direct conversion of generated videos to 4D scene representations
- Inverse dynamic model learning for embodied agents

### 6. Unified Multimodal Generative Models Survey

Hu, Y., Wang, L., Liu, X., Chen, L.-H., Guo, Y., Shi, Y., Liu, C., Rao, A., Wang, Z., & Xiong, H. (2025). *Simulating the Real World: A Unified Survey of Multimodal Generative Models*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2503.04641. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04641v3

**Summary:** This unified survey addresses the fragmentation in multimodal generative models by presenting a framework investigating progression of data dimensionality in real-world simulation. The survey progresses from 2D generation (appearance) to video (appearance+dynamics), 3D generation (appearance+geometry), and culminates in 4D generation integrating all dimensions. This represents the first systematic unification of 2D, video, 3D, and 4D generation within a single framework. The survey provides comprehensive review of datasets, evaluation metrics, and future directions, serving as a bridge to advance multimodal generative models and real-world simulation.

**Key Contributions:**
- First unified framework connecting 2D, video, 3D, and 4D generation
- Systematic integration of interdependencies between modalities
- Comprehensive resource for datasets, metrics, and research directions

### 7. 4D Driving World Model

Liang, D., Zhang, D., Zhou, X., Tu, S., Feng, T., Li, X., Zhang, Y., Du, M., Tan, X., & Bai, X. (2025). *UniFuture: A 4D Driving World Model for Future Generation and Perception*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2503.13587. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13587v2

**Summary:** UniFuture presents a unified 4D Driving World Model that bridges appearance and geometry, contrasting with existing models focusing solely on 2D video generation (lacking geometry) or static perception (lacking temporal dynamics). The approach treats future RGB images and depth maps as coupled projections of 4D reality, modeled jointly via Dual-Latent Sharing (DLS) mapping visual and geometric modalities into shared spatio-temporal latent space. Multi-scale Latent Interaction (MLI) enforces bidirectional consistency: geometry constrains visual synthesis, while visual semantics refine geometric estimation. Experiments on nuScenes and Waymo demonstrate superior performance in both future generation and geometry perception.

**Key Contributions:**
- Unified framework coupling appearance and geometry in 4D driving scenes
- Dual-Latent Sharing scheme for spatio-temporal latent representation
- Multi-scale Latent Interaction for bidirectional consistency

### 8. General 3D and 4D Scene Generation

Zhao, Y., Lin, C.-C., Lin, K., Yan, Z., Li, L., Yang, Z., Wang, J., Lee, G. H., & Wang, L. (2024). *GenXD: Generating Any 3D and 4D Scenes*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2411.02319. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02319v2

**Summary:** GenXD addresses challenges in 3D and 4D generation stemming from lack of large-scale 4D data and effective model design. The framework leverages camera and object movements from daily life observations. A data curation pipeline extracts camera poses and object motion strength from videos, creating CamVid-30K, a large-scale real-world 4D scene dataset. Multiview-temporal modules disentangle camera and object movements to learn from both 3D and 4D data. Masked latent conditions support various conditioning views, enabling generation of videos following camera trajectories and consistent 3D views liftable into 3D representations.

**Key Contributions:**
- CamVid-30K: Large-scale real-world 4D scene dataset
- Multiview-temporal modules disentangling camera and object motion
- Versatile framework supporting both 3D and 4D generation

### 9. Single-Image 3D and 4D Scene Generation

Sun, W., Chen, S., Liu, F., Chen, Z., Duan, Y., Zhang, J., & Wang, Y. (2024). *DimensionX: Create Any 3D and 4D Scenes from a Single Image with Controllable Video Diffusion*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2411.04928. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04928v1

**Summary:** DimensionX introduces a framework for generating photorealistic 3D and 4D scenes from single images using video diffusion. The approach represents both 3D spatial structure and 4D temporal evolution through video frame sequences. ST-Director decouples spatial and temporal factors in video diffusion by learning dimension-aware LoRAs from dimension-variant data, enabling precise manipulation of spatial structure and temporal dynamics. A trajectory-aware mechanism bridges generated videos and real-world scenes for 3D generation, while identity-preserving denoising supports 4D generation. Experiments demonstrate superior results in controllable video generation and 3D/4D scene generation.

**Key Contributions:**
- ST-Director: Dimension-aware LoRA learning for spatial-temporal decoupling
- Single-image to 3D/4D scene generation pipeline
- Trajectory-aware and identity-preserving mechanisms for quality improvement

### 10. Efficient 4D Scene Forecasting

Liao, Z., Wei, P., Zhang, R., Chen, S., Wang, H., & Ren, Z. (2025). *I²-World: Intra-Inter Tokenization for Efficient Dynamic 4D Scene Forecasting*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2507.09144. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09144v2

**Summary:** I²-World addresses efficient tokenization of complex 3D scenes for occupancy-based world models in autonomous driving. The framework decouples scene tokenization into intra-scene and inter-scene tokenizers. The intra-scene tokenizer employs multi-scale residual quantization to hierarchically compress 3D scenes while preserving spatial details. The inter-scene tokenizer residually aggregates temporal dependencies across timesteps. Unlike decoder-only GPT-style autoregressive models, I²-World adopts encoder-decoder architecture with encoder predicting transformation matrix for high-level control, and decoder ensuring temporal consistency. The method achieves state-of-the-art performance with 25.1% improvement in mIoU and 36.9% in IoU, requiring only 2.9 GB training memory and achieving 37.0 FPS real-time inference.

**Key Contributions:**
- Dual tokenizer architecture: intra-scene and inter-scene separation
- Encoder-decoder alternative to GPT-style autoregressive models
- Exceptional efficiency: 2.9 GB memory, 37.0 FPS inference

## Thematic Analysis

### Theme 1: World Simulators for Autonomous Driving

Four papers focus specifically on autonomous driving applications: OccSora (Wang et al., 2024), UniFuture (Liang et al., 2025), I²-World (Liao et al., 2025), and the survey by Hu et al. (2025). These works emphasize occupancy-based representations, trajectory-conditioned generation, and real-time efficiency. Common challenges include long-term temporal consistency, computational efficiency, and integration of geometric constraints.

### Theme 2: Compositional and Hierarchical Approaches

Three papers advocate compositional architectures: CityDreamer4D (Xie et al., 2025) separates static and dynamic city components, GenXD (Zhao et al., 2024) disentangles camera and object motion, and the 4D Spatial Intelligence survey (Cao et al., 2025) proposes five-level hierarchical framework. This theme reflects recognition that different scene elements require specialized modeling strategies.

### Theme 3: Multimodal Integration

Two papers emphasize multimodal integration: 4D LangSplat (Li et al., 2025) integrates language with 4D Gaussian representations via MLLMs, and TesserAct (Zhen et al., 2025) incorporates RGB-DN (depth and normal) for embodied prediction. The unified survey (Hu et al., 2025) bridges 2D, video, 3D, and 4D modalities within single framework.

### Theme 4: Efficiency and Scalability

Three papers address computational efficiency: I²-World (Liao et al., 2025) achieves real-time 37.0 FPS inference, OccSora (Wang et al., 2024) uses diffusion for efficient long-sequence modeling, and DimensionX (Sun et al., 2024) employs dimension-aware LoRAs for controllable generation. This theme reflects practical deployment requirements for real-world applications.

## Key Challenges Identified

1. **Temporal Consistency:** Maintaining coherent evolution across time remains challenging, especially for long sequences (Wang et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2025)

2. **Data Scarcity:** Lack of large-scale real-world 4D datasets limits model training (Zhao et al., 2024; Cao et al., 2025)

3. **Computational Efficiency:** Balancing quality with real-time inference requirements for deployment (Liao et al., 2025)

4. **Multimodal Integration:** Effectively combining appearance, geometry, dynamics, and semantics (Hu et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025)

5. **Physical Realism:** Incorporating physical laws and constraints for embodied interaction (Cao et al., 2025; Zhen et al., 2025)

## Future Directions

Based on the reviewed literature, promising research directions include:

1. **Higher Levels of Spatial Intelligence:** Advancing from Level 3 (dynamic scenes) to Levels 4-5 (interaction modeling and physical constraints) as outlined by Cao et al. (2025)

2. **Unified Multimodal Frameworks:** Developing integrated approaches that jointly model 2D, video, 3D, and 4D representations (Hu et al., 2025)

3. **Efficient Tokenization:** Improving compression and representation of 4D scenes for real-time applications (Liao et al., 2025)

4. **Language-Grounded 4D Understanding:** Extending open-vocabulary queries to dynamic scenes with temporal reasoning (Li et al., 2025)

5. **Embodied World Models:** Enhancing prediction of scene evolution in response to agent actions for robotics applications (Zhen et al., 2025)

## Conclusion

The reviewed literature demonstrates rapid advancement in 3D and 4D world modeling, with significant progress in autonomous driving simulators, compositional generation, multimodal integration, and computational efficiency. The field is moving toward unified frameworks that integrate appearance, geometry, dynamics, and semantics while maintaining practical efficiency for real-world deployment. Key challenges remain in temporal consistency, data availability, and physical realism, offering rich opportunities for future research.
