## Executive Summary

This systematic literature review examines recent advances in 3D and 4D world modeling, focusing on papers published between September 2023 and September 2025 in the computer vision category. The selected papers represent cutting-edge research in dynamic scene reconstruction, generative modeling, autonomous driving simulation, and embodied AI. Key themes include: (1) 4D scene generation and representation, (2) world models for autonomous driving, (3) multimodal and compositional approaches, and (4) efficient tokenization and forecasting methods.

## Paper Reviews (APA Format)

### 1. 4D Language Gaussian Splatting

Li, W., Zhou, R., Zhou, J., Song, Y., Herter, J., Qin, M., Huang, G., & Pfister, H. (2025). *4D LangSplat: 4D Language Gaussian Splatting via Multimodal Large Language Models*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2503.10437. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10437v2

**Summary:** This paper addresses the challenge of learning 4D language fields for time-sensitive, open-ended language queries in dynamic scenes. The authors propose 4D LangSplat, which learns 4D language fields directly from text generated by Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) rather than from vision features. The key innovation is a multimodal object-wise video prompting method that guides MLLMs to generate detailed, temporally consistent captions for objects throughout a video. These captions are encoded using a Large Language Model into high-quality sentence embeddings for open-vocabulary text queries. A status deformable network models continuous changes over time. Results demonstrate precise and efficient performance for both time-sensitive and time-agnostic open-vocabulary queries in dynamic scenes.

**Key Contributions:**
- Novel approach to 4D language field learning via MLLM-generated captions
- Multimodal object-wise video prompting for temporally consistent captions
- Status deformable network for modeling smooth temporal transitions
- Efficient handling of dynamic scenes with evolving object semantics

### 2. 4D Spatial Intelligence Survey

Cao, Y., Lu, J., Huang, Z., Shen, Z., Zhao, C., Hong, F., Chen, Z., Li, X., Wang, W., Liu, Y., & Liu, Z. (2025). *Reconstructing 4D Spatial Intelligence: A Survey*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2507.21045. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21045v2

**Summary:** This comprehensive survey organizes 4D spatial intelligence reconstruction methods into five progressive levels: (1) reconstruction of low-level 3D attributes (depth, pose, point maps), (2) reconstruction of 3D scene components (objects, humans, structures), (3) reconstruction of 4D dynamic scenes, (4) modeling of interactions among scene components, and (5) incorporation of physical laws and constraints. The authors provide a hierarchical analysis that addresses the gap in existing surveys, which rarely offer comprehensive structural analysis of 4D scene reconstruction. The survey covers applications ranging from entertainment (movies) to embodied AI (interaction modeling and physical realism). Key challenges at each level are discussed, along with promising directions for advancing toward richer levels of 4D spatial intelligence.

**Key Contributions:**
- First hierarchical framework organizing 4D reconstruction into five progressive levels
- Comprehensive analysis spanning from low-level attributes to physical law incorporation
- Identification of key challenges and future directions at each level
- Bridge between entertainment applications and embodied AI requirements

### 3. Compositional 4D City Generation

Xie, H., Chen, Z., Hong, F., & Liu, Z. (2025). *Compositional Generative Model of Unbounded 4D Cities*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2501.08983. https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08983v4

**Summary:** This paper presents CityDreamer4D, a compositional generative model specifically designed for generating unbounded 4D cities. The authors address two key insights: (1) 4D city generation should separate dynamic objects (vehicles) from static scenes (buildings, roads), and (2) all objects should be composed of different types of neural fields for buildings, vehicles, and background. The approach includes a Traffic Scenario Generator and Unbounded Layout Generator that produce dynamic traffic scenarios and static city layouts using compact BEV representation. Objects are generated by combining stuff-oriented and instance-oriented neural fields with customized generative hash grids and periodic positional embeddings. The model supports downstream applications including instance editing, city stylization, and urban simulation, delivering state-of-the-art performance in realistic 4D city generation.

**Key Contributions:**
- Compositional design separating dynamic and static city elements
- Traffic Scenario Generator and Unbounded Layout Generator for comprehensive city generation
- Customized neural fields for different object types (buildings, vehicles, background)
- Comprehensive dataset suite including OSM, GoogleEarth, and CityTopia

### 4. 4D Occupancy World Simulator for Autonomous Driving

Wang, L., Zheng, W., Ren, Y., Jiang, H., Cui, Z., Yu, H., & Lu, J. (2024). *OccSora: 4D Occupancy Generation Models as World Simulators for Autonomous Driving*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2405.20337. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20337v1

**Summary:** OccSora proposes a diffusion-based 4D occupancy generation model to simulate 3D world development for autonomous driving. Unlike conventional methods that model scene development with individual instance motion or autoregressive frameworks that suffer from inefficiency in long-term temporal modeling, OccSora employs a 4D scene tokenizer to obtain compact discrete spatial-temporal representations. A diffusion transformer is trained on these representations to generate 4D occupancy conditioned on trajectory prompts. Experiments on the nuScenes dataset with Occ3D occupancy annotations demonstrate that OccSora can generate 16-second videos with authentic 3D layout and temporal consistency. The model shows potential as a world simulator for autonomous driving decision-making with trajectory-aware 4D generation.

**Key Contributions:**
- Diffusion-based approach overcoming autoregressive inefficiency for long-term temporal modeling
- 4D scene tokenizer for compact discrete spatial-temporal representations
- High-quality reconstruction for long-sequence occupancy videos
- Trajectory-aware 4D generation for autonomous driving simulation

### 5. 4D Embodied World Models

Zhen, H., Sun, Q., Zhang, H., Li, J., Zhou, S., Du, Y., & Gan, C. (2025). *TesserAct: Learning 4D Embodied World Models*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2504.20995. https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20995v1

**Summary:** This paper presents an approach for learning 4D embodied world models that predict dynamic evolution of 3D scenes over time in response to embodied agent actions, providing both spatial and temporal consistency. The method trains on RGB-DN (RGB, Depth, and Normal) videos, surpassing traditional 2D models by incorporating detailed shape, configuration, and temporal changes. The authors extend existing robotic manipulation video datasets with depth and normal information using off-the-shelf models, then fine-tune a video generation model to jointly predict RGB-DN for each frame. An algorithm directly converts generated RGB-DN videos into high-quality 4D scenes. The method ensures temporal and spatial coherence, enables novel view synthesis for embodied environments, and facilitates policy learning that significantly outperforms prior video-based world models.

**Key Contributions:**
- RGB-DN video training for enhanced 3D scene prediction with temporal dynamics
- Extension of robotic manipulation datasets with depth and normal information
- Direct conversion of RGB-DN videos to high-quality 4D scenes
- Superior policy learning performance compared to video-based world models

### 6. Unified Multimodal Generative Models Survey

Hu, Y., Wang, L., Liu, X., Chen, L.-H., Guo, Y., Shi, Y., Liu, C., Rao, A., Wang, Z., & Xiong, H. (2025). *Simulating the Real World: A Unified Survey of Multimodal Generative Models*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2503.04641. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04641v3

**Summary:** This unified survey investigates the progression of data dimensionality in real-world simulation, presenting a comprehensive framework that systematically integrates 2D, video, 3D, and 4D generation. The survey progresses from 2D generation (appearance), to video (appearance + dynamics), to 3D generation (appearance + geometry), and culminates in 4D generation integrating all dimensions. This represents the first attempt to systematically unify the study of different dimensional generation within a single framework, addressing the limitation that current methods often treat different modalities as independent domains. The survey provides comprehensive review of datasets, evaluation metrics, and future directions, serving as a bridge to advance multimodal generative models and real-world simulation research.

**Key Contributions:**
- First unified framework systematically integrating 2D, video, 3D, and 4D generation
- Progressive dimensional analysis from appearance to full spatiotemporal integration
- Comprehensive datasets and evaluation metrics review
- Bridge for newcomers to multimodal generative model research

### 7. 4D Driving World Model

Liang, D., Zhang, D., Zhou, X., Tu, S., Feng, T., Li, X., Zhang, Y., Du, M., Tan, X., & Bai, X. (2025). *UniFuture: A 4D Driving World Model for Future Generation and Perception*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2503.13587. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13587v2

**Summary:** UniFuture presents a unified 4D Driving World Model designed to simulate dynamic evolution of the 3D physical world, bridging appearance and geometry to construct holistic 4D representation. Unlike existing driving world models focusing solely on 2D pixel-level video generation (lacking geometry) or static perception (lacking temporal dynamics), UniFuture treats future RGB images and depth maps as coupled projections of the same 4D reality. The approach introduces Dual-Latent Sharing (DLS) mapping visual and geometric modalities into a shared spatio-temporal latent space, and Multi-scale Latent Interaction (MLI) enforcing bidirectional consistency where geometry constrains visual synthesis and visual semantics refine geometric estimation. Experiments on nuScenes and Waymo datasets demonstrate superior performance in both future generation and geometry perception.

**Key Contributions:**
- Unified framework bridging appearance and geometry for 4D driving simulation
- Dual-Latent Sharing scheme for shared spatio-temporal latent space
- Multi-scale Latent Interaction for bidirectional geometry-visual consistency
- State-of-the-art performance on nuScenes and Waymo datasets

### 8. General 3D and 4D Scene Generation

Zhao, Y., Lin, C.-C., Lin, K., Yan, Z., Li, L., Yang, Z., Wang, J., Lee, G. H., & Wang, L. (2024). *GenXD: Generating Any 3D and 4D Scenes*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2411.02319. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02319v2

**Summary:** GenXD addresses the challenge of 3D and 4D generation by jointly investigating general 3D and 4D scene generation leveraging camera and object movements from daily life. Due to lack of real-world 4D data, the authors propose a data curation pipeline to obtain camera poses and object motion strength from videos, introducing CamVid-30K, a large-scale real-world 4D scene dataset. The framework employs multiview-temporal modules that disentangle camera and object movements to seamlessly learn from both 3D and 4D data. GenXD uses masked latent conditions to support various conditioning views, generating videos following camera trajectories and consistent 3D views liftable into 3D representations. Extensive evaluations demonstrate effectiveness and versatility compared to previous methods.

**Key Contributions:**
- Joint 3D and 4D generation framework with disentangled camera-object movements
- CamVid-30K large-scale real-world 4D scene dataset
- Multiview-temporal modules for seamless 3D/4D learning
- Versatile conditioning supporting multiple view types

### 9. Single-Image 3D and 4D Scene Generation

Sun, W., Chen, S., Liu, F., Chen, Z., Duan, Y., Zhang, J., & Wang, Y. (2024). *DimensionX: Create Any 3D and 4D Scenes from a Single Image with Controllable Video Diffusion*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2411.04928. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04928v1

**Summary:** DimensionX introduces a framework for generating photorealistic 3D and 4D scenes from single images using video diffusion. The approach recognizes that both 3D spatial structure and 4D temporal evolution can be represented through video frame sequences. To overcome limitations of video diffusion models in spatial and temporal controllability, the authors propose ST-Director, which decouples spatial and temporal factors by learning dimension-aware LoRAs from dimension-variant data. This enables precise manipulation of spatial structure and temporal dynamics. A trajectory-aware mechanism for 3D generation and identity-preserving denoising strategy for 4D generation bridge the gap between generated videos and real-world scenes. Extensive experiments demonstrate superior results in controllable video generation and 3D/4D scene generation.

**Key Contributions:**
- ST-Director decoupling spatial and temporal factors in video diffusion
- Dimension-aware LoRAs for precise spatial-temporal manipulation
- Single-image to 3D/4D scene generation pipeline
- Trajectory-aware and identity-preserving mechanisms for realism

### 10. Efficient 4D Scene Forecasting

Liao, Z., Wei, P., Zhang, R., Chen, S., Wang, H., & Ren, Z. (2025). *I²-World: Intra-Inter Tokenization for Efficient Dynamic 4D Scene Forecasting*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2507.09144. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09144v2

**Summary:** I²-World proposes an efficient framework for 4D occupancy forecasting addressing the challenge of tokenizing complex 3D scenes. The method decouples scene tokenization into intra-scene and inter-scene tokenizers. The intra-scene tokenizer employs multi-scale residual quantization to hierarchically compress 3D scenes while preserving spatial details. The inter-scene tokenizer residually aggregates temporal dependencies across timesteps. Unlike decoder-only GPT-style autoregressive models, I²-World adopts an encoder-decoder architecture where the encoder aggregates spatial context and predicts a transformation matrix for high-level control, and the decoder ensures temporal consistency. Experiments demonstrate state-of-the-art performance, outperforming existing methods by 25.1% in mIoU and 36.9% in IoU for 4D occupancy forecasting, with exceptional efficiency: 2.9 GB training memory and 37.0 FPS real-time inference.

**Key Contributions:**
- Dual tokenization design: intra-scene (spatial) and inter-scene (temporal)
- Multi-scale residual quantization for hierarchical 3D compression
- Encoder-decoder architecture with transformation matrix control
- State-of-the-art performance with exceptional computational efficiency

## Thematic Analysis

### Major Research Themes

**1. 4D Scene Representation and Generation**
Six papers focus on 4D scene representation and generation (Li et al., 2025; Xie et al., 2025; Zhen et al., 2025; Zhao et al., 2024; Sun et al., 2024; Liao et al., 2025). Common approaches include Gaussian splatting, neural fields, diffusion models, and tokenization-based methods. The trend shows movement toward more efficient and controllable representations.

**2. Autonomous Driving Applications**
Three papers specifically address autonomous driving applications (Wang et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2025; Liao et al., 2025). These works emphasize occupancy forecasting, trajectory-aware generation, and geometric consistency, reflecting the practical importance of world models for safe autonomous navigation.

**3. Survey and Unification Efforts**
Two comprehensive surveys provide frameworks for understanding the field (Cao et al., 2025; Hu et al., 2025). Cao et al. propose a five-level hierarchy for 4D spatial intelligence, while Hu et al. unify 2D, video, 3D, and 4D generation within a single framework. These surveys indicate the field's maturation and need for systematic organization.

**4. Multimodal Integration**
Multiple papers integrate multiple modalities: language (Li et al., 2025), depth and normals (Zhen et al., 2025), RGB-D (Liang et al., 2025). This trend reflects recognition that comprehensive world modeling requires rich multimodal understanding.

**5. Efficiency and Scalability**
Recent work emphasizes computational efficiency (Liao et al., 2025 report 2.9 GB training memory and 37 FPS), compositional approaches for scalability (Xie et al., 2025), and large-scale dataset curation (Zhao et al., 2024). These advances address practical deployment challenges.

### Methodological Trends

**Generative Approaches:** Diffusion models dominate recent work (Wang et al., 2024; Sun et al., 2024), offering advantages over autoregressive methods for long-term temporal modeling.

**Neural Representations:** Neural fields, Gaussian splatting, and learned tokenizers provide flexible, differentiable scene representations supporting various downstream tasks.

**Compositional Design:** Separating static/dynamic elements (Xie et al., 2025) or spatial/temporal factors (Sun et al., 2024; Liao et al., 2025) enables more controllable and interpretable generation.

**Self-Supervised Learning:** Several methods leverage pre-trained models (MLLMs, depth estimators, normal predictors) to reduce annotation requirements and improve generalization.

## Research Gaps and Future Directions

### Identified Gaps

1. **Physical Realism:** While Cao et al. (2025) identify physical law incorporation as Level 5 of 4D intelligence, few papers explicitly model physics. Most methods focus on visual and geometric consistency without explicit physical constraints.

2. **Interaction Modeling:** Despite embodied AI applications, limited work addresses complex multi-agent interactions and causal reasoning in 4D scenes.

3. **Evaluation Standards:** Hu et al. (2025) note inconsistent evaluation metrics across studies. Standardized benchmarks for 4D world modeling remain underdeveloped.

4. **Long-Horizon Prediction:** While OccSora (Wang et al., 2024) achieves 16-second generation and I²-World (Liao et al., 2025) demonstrates efficiency, predicting far-future states with accuracy remains challenging.

5. **Data Scarcity:** Multiple papers note limited real-world 4D data (Zhao et al., 2024; Xie et al., 2025), requiring synthetic data or curation pipelines that may introduce biases.

### Promising Directions

1. **Integration with Large Language Models:** Li et al. (2025) demonstrate MLLMs' potential for semantic understanding in 4D scenes. Future work may leverage LLMs for reasoning, planning, and causal understanding.

2. **Neuro-Symbolic Approaches:** Combining neural representations with symbolic reasoning could address physical realism and causal understanding gaps.

3. **Embodied Learning:** Zhen et al. (2025) show embodied world models' advantages for policy learning. Tighter integration with robotic learning could accelerate progress.

4. **Efficient Architectures:** I²-World's efficiency (Liao et al., 2025) suggests transformer and tokenizer innovations will enable real-time applications.

5. **Unified Frameworks:** Hu et al. (2025) and Cao et al. (2025) call for unified approaches spanning dimensionalities and intelligence levels. Future work may develop more integrated architectures.

---

## Conclusion

The 3D and 4D world modeling field has advanced rapidly between 2023-2025, with significant progress in representation learning, generative modeling, and practical applications. The 10 papers reviewed demonstrate diverse approaches including diffusion models, neural fields, Gaussian splatting, and tokenization methods. Key application domains include autonomous driving, embodied AI, and urban simulation.

Major trends include movement toward unified frameworks bridging appearance and geometry, compositional designs separating spatial/temporal or static/dynamic factors, and emphasis on computational efficiency for real-time applications. Two comprehensive surveys provide frameworks for understanding the field's hierarchical structure and dimensional progression.

Critical challenges remain in physical realism, interaction modeling, evaluation standardization, and long-horizon prediction. Future research directions include LLM integration, neuro-symbolic approaches, embodied learning, and unified architectures spanning multiple dimensionalities and intelligence levels.

The field shows strong momentum toward AGI capabilities for understanding and simulating the real world, with practical applications in autonomous systems, robotics, and content generation.
